“Intense World is built for people who want to train consistently, improve their strength and become a stronger version of themselves.”

Founded in 2024 near Jeevanjaneyaswamy Temple in Kote, Chikkaballapur, and now expanded to our second state-of-the-art facility in Shidlaghatta — Intense World was created to provide a serious, high-standard training ground. We focus on clean floors, solid weights, reliable machines, and coaches who give genuine advice.
